                                           IMMIGRATION ACT, 2002
                                             (Act No. 13 of 2002)
                                                     [Section 15(1A)]



 LIST      UNDESIRABLE BUSINESS
           OF                                                 UNDERTAKING                IN       RELATION TO AN
 APPLICATION FOR BUSINESS VISA


         M K N Gigaba, MP, Minister of Home Affairs have, in terms of section 15(1A) of the
 Immigration Act, 2002 (Act No. 13 of 2002) and after consultation with the Minister of Trade
 and Industry, list the following business undertaking to be undesirable in relation to an
 application for a business visa:
 (a)     Businesses that import second hand motor vehicles into the Republic of South Africa for
        the purpose of exporting to other markets outside the Republic of South Africa.
 (b)    The exotic entertainment industry.
 (c)    Security Industry.
