                FIRST AMENDMENT OF THE REGULATIONS ON FEES MADE UNDER THE
                                               IMMIGRATION ACT, 2002


               I, Dr Leon Amos Schreiber, MP, Minister of Home Affairs, have, in terms of section
               7(1)(i) of the Immigration Act, 2002 (Act No. 13 of 2002), after consultation with the
