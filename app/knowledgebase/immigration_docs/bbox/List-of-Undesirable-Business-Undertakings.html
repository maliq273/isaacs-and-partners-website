4 No. 37837 GOVERNMENT GAZETTE, 15 JULY 2014 No. 561 15 July 2014 IMMIGRATION ACT, 2002 (Act No. 13 of 2002) [Section 15(1A)] UNDESIRABLE BUSINESS APPLICATION FOR BUSINESS VISA LIST OF UNDERTAKING IN RELATION TO AN M K N Gigaba, MP, Minister of Home Affairs have, in terms of section 15(1A) of the Immigration Act, 2002 (Act No. 13 of 2002) and after consultation with the Minister of Trade and Industry, list the following business undertaking to be undesirable in relation to an application for a business visa: (a) Businesses that import second hand motor vehicles into the Republic of South Africa for the purpose of exporting to other markets outside the Republic of South Africa.
